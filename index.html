    How to play
    • Each side has an immortal snail that pursues its own king.
    • Every three moves of a color, only its snail activates. The counters are independent.
    • It advances one square in any of the eight directions, choosing the shortest Chebyshev distance.
    • In case of a tie, the configured order is used. By default: column first, then row, in ascending order.
    • Upon reaching a square, it removes any piece located there. Pieces neither block nor capture snails.
    • If it reaches its own king, that side loses. If it captures the other king, it wins.
    • A piece can occupy a square containing a snail. The capture is only resolved when the snail activates.
    • If it is already adjacent to the king, it reaches him. If it shares his square, it remains there and captures him upon activating.
    • A move cannot leave its own king in check after the activation, unless the activation has already ended the game.
    • Snail endings take priority over checkmate or draw produced on that same move.
    • Conventional rules for promotion, castling, and en passant are maintained. Conventional endings also apply.

    The AI simulates the activations and immediate winning responses. Stockfish evaluates the resulting positions, but does not know this variant on its own. Optimal play is not guaranteed.
